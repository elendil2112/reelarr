# Bundled data

- `places.tsv.gz` — every city with 1,000+ people, derived from
  [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org) `cities1000`. © GeoNames, licensed
  under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Rebuilt with `tools/build_places.py`.
- `countries.tsv` — ISO 3166 country names via
  [pycountry](https://github.com/pycountry/pycountry), with everyday spellings
  ("South Korea", "Czech Republic") and common aliases added by Reelarr.
